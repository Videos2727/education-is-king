Online lessons by year group, subject and topic

Welcome

This website lets students access lessons by year group, subject and topic. PowerPoint lessons are shown as slide images, so students do not receive the original PowerPoint file.

Important: This free version makes downloading harder, but students can still screenshot slides. Do not upload original PowerPoint files for students.

How students use it

1. Choose year group

2. Choose subject

3. Choose topic lesson

4. View slides and answer questions
